# VALTARIS LOGO SPECIFICATION

## Primary Symbol
A sharp, angular "V" in VALTARIS Blue (#1A4FFF).

## Half-Star Geometry
Two VALTARIS Gold (#F5C542) half-stars are embedded into the V:
- Left side: right half of a star, cut along the V’s left diagonal.
- Right side: left half of a star, cut along the V’s right diagonal.

The V slices the stars, creating a propulsion and aerospace effect.

## Wordmark
VALTARIS in uppercase, using an aerospace/tech font.
Color: VALTARIS Blue (#1A4FFF).

## Special Feature
The dot on the "i" is a VALTARIS Gold half-star, cut to match the V’s geometry.

## Color Palette
- VALTARIS Blue: #1A4FFF
- VALTARIS Gold: #F5C542
- Deep Space Black: #0A0A0F
- Satellite Gray: #1F1F27

## Brand Identity
Precision, aerospace, enterprise, velocity, excellence.
